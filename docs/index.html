FROM RECORDING TO REHEARSAL

Your favorite songs.
A little more personal.

≋

Pull the song apart.

Import a recording and separate lead vocals, backing vocals, drums, bass, guitar, piano, and more. Keep the parts you love.

♭

Make it fit your voice.

Change key and speed independently. Overlapping harmonies can follow the lead level, while other backing vocals keep their own.

⊞

Keep every version.

Save a practice mix and a performance mix. Put the same song in several collections, with its cover right where it belongs.

↗

Pass the mic around.

Let friends join the room, queue songs, and share duet parts. One Stage screen plays the music; the other screens control it.
